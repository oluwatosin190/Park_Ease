# Quick Start Guide - Reviews Page

## 🎯 Accessing the Reviews Page

### From Navigation Bar
1. Go to **Homepage** (`http://localhost/Park_Ease/parkease/`)
2. Click **"Reviews"** in the top navigation bar
3. You'll be taken to the reviews page

### From Dashboard
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Dashboard** (`http://localhost/Park_Ease/parkease/dashboard.php`)
3. Click **"Reviews"** in the sidebar menu
4. You'll be taken to the reviews page

### Direct Access
Visit: `http://localhost/Park_Ease/reviews.php`

---

## 📖 Browsing Reviews

### View All Reviews
- The page displays all community reviews by default
- Each review shows:
  - Parking lot name
  - Reviewer name
  - Review date
  - Star rating (1-5)
  - Review text

### Search for Reviews
1. Type in the **search box** (parking name, reviewer, keywords)
2. Click **"Search"** button
3. Results update automatically

### Filter by Rating
1. Select a **rating** from the dropdown (1-5 stars)
2. Click **"Search"** button
3. Only reviews with that rating will show

### Sort Reviews
1. Select from **sort options**:
   - **Newest First** (default)
   - **Oldest First**
   - **Highest Rated**
   - **Lowest Rated**
2. Click **"Search"** button

### Navigate Pages
- Reviews are shown **10 per page**
- Use pagination buttons: **First**, **Previous**, **Next**, **Last**
- Or click on a specific page number

### Clear Filters
- Click **"Clear all filters"** link to reset search

---

## ✍️ Submitting a Review

### Before You Start
- You must be **signed in** to submit a review
- If not signed in, click the **"Sign in"** link to log in
- Or **"Create one here"** to register

### Submit a Review
1. **Select a Parking Lot**
   - Choose from the dropdown list

2. **Choose Your Rating**
   - Select 1-5 stars based on your experience

3. **Write Your Review**
   - Minimum **10 characters** required
   - Share your detailed experience
   - Be specific and helpful

4. **Post Review**
   - Click **"Post Review"** button
   - You'll see a success message
   - Review appears in the list

---

## 📊 Statistics Dashboard

At the top of the page, you'll see:

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Can I edit my review after posting?
A: Currently, reviews cannot be edited. If you need to change it, contact support.

### Q: How long does it take for my review to appear?
A: Your review appears immediately after posting.

### Q: Can I delete my review?
A: Contact the support team to request deletion.

### Q: What if I see an inappropriate review?
A: Use the report function or contact the support team.

### Q: Is my information private?
A: Your email is private. Only your name appears in reviews.

### Q: Can I submit multiple reviews for the same parking lot?
A: Yes, you can submit multiple reviews.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

- Your review is too short
- Add more details

### "Please fill in all fields correctly"
- Make sure all fields are completed
- Select a parking lot
- Select a rating
- Enter review text

### Page not loading
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
